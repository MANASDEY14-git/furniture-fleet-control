## 2. Regulatory Landscape and HSN Classification for Furniture

Under the Harmonized System of Nomenclature (HSN), the vast majority of furniture, bedding, mattresses, and cushions fall under **Chapter 94** [5]. Proper classification is the foundational prerequisite for correct tax computation within the ERP calculation engine.

### 2.1 HSN Code Hierarchy and GST Rate Structure

The standard tax rate applicable to most commercial, domestic, and institutional furniture in India is **18%** (comprising 9% Central GST and 9% State GST for intra-state supplies, or 18% Integrated GST for inter-state transactions) [1] [11]. However, specific sub-categories and constituent materials dictate nuanced statutory rates.

| HSN Code | Description | Standard GST Rate | Statutory Breakdown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **9401** | Seats (other than those of heading 9402), whether or not convertible into beds, and parts thereof | 18% | 9% CGST + 9% SGST / 18% IGST |
| **9403** | Other furniture and parts thereof (wooden, metal, plastic, office, and modular furniture) | 18% | 9% CGST + 9% SGST / 18% IGST |
| **9403 80** | Furniture of bamboo, rattan, cane, or similar materials | 12% | 6% CGST + 6% SGST / 12% IGST |
| **9404** | Mattress supports; articles of bedding and similar furnishing (mattresses, quilts, cushions) | 12% / 18% | Dependent on sale value and composition |
| **9403 90** | Parts of furniture (metal, wood, plastic fittings) | 18% | 9% CGST + 9% SGST / 18% IGST |

> "The GST rate applicable to furniture parts under HSN 94039000 remains 18% following rationalisation changes, ensuring alignment with assembled and unassembled furniture modules." [9]

## 3. Operational Dynamics: Wholesale (B2B) vs. Retail (B2C & B2B)

Furniture businesses operate across distinct distribution channels, each imposing unique compliance mandates upon the ERP billing and logistics sub-systems.

### 3.1 Wholesale (B2B) Operations

Wholesale transactions involve bulk sales to independent retailers, corporate offices, interior designers, and institutional buyers.

- **Mandatory Invoicing Fields:** Every B2B invoice generated by the ERP must capture the recipient's GSTIN, Trade Name, State Code, Place of Supply (PoS), HSN code, taxable value, and itemized tax splits.
- **E-Invoicing Applicability:** Enterprises with an Aggregate Annual Turnover (AATO) exceeding the statutory threshold (currently established at **₹5 crore and above**) are legally mandated to generate an Invoice Reference Number (IRN) and digitally signed QR code via the Invoice Registration Portal (IRP) in real-time [3].
- **E-Way Bill Integration:** For any consignment of furniture whose taxable/invoice value exceeds **₹50,000**, the ERP must automatically trigger Part A and Part B generation on the E-Way Bill portal before physical dispatch [7].

### 3.2 Retail (B2C & B2B) Operations

Retail showrooms and direct-to-consumer (D2C) platforms handle high-volume, low-value transactions with unregistered walk-in consumers (B2C) alongside registered B2B walk-ins.

- **B2C Invoicing:** For transactions with unregistered end-consumers, a standard retail tax invoice is issued. If aggregate B2C turnover exceeds statutory electronic payment thresholds, dynamic QR codes must be embedded on the invoice.
- **Point of Sale (POS) Integration:** The ERP retail module must instantly record cash, card, and digital wallet settlements while adjusting warehouse inventory ledgers and segregating taxable turnover for monthly GSTR-1 (Table 7 - B2CS) filing.

## 4. Input Tax Credit (ITC), Reverse Charge, and GSTR-2B Reconciliation

Maintaining optimal working capital in the furniture industry depends on flawless Input Tax Credit (ITC) capture on raw material procurement (plywood, hardware, foam, fabric) and finished goods purchases.

### 4.1 ITC Eligibility and Conditions

Under Section 16 of the CGST Act, a registered taxable person is entitled to claim ITC on goods or services used in the course or furtherance of business, subject to the following statutory prerequisites:

1. Possession of a valid tax invoice or debit note issued by a registered supplier.
2. Receipt of goods or services (verified via Goods Receipt Notes in the ERP inventory module).
3. Filing of returns by the supplier, causing the invoice to auto-populate in the enterprise's **GSTR-2B** statement.
4. Payment of tax charged by the supplier to the government exchequer.

### 4.2 GSTR-2B Automated Reconciliation Engine

An enterprise-grade ERP must feature an automated reconciliation daemon that polls the GSTN API periodically to fetch GSTR-2B data. The engine categorizes purchase invoices into four distinct states:

- **Matched:** ERP purchase register perfectly matches GSTR-2B records (Amount, GSTIN, Invoice Number). ITC is fully claimable.
- **Mismatched:** Discrepancies found in taxable value or tax amount. The ERP must flag these for vendor follow-up and restrict immediate ITC claiming.
- **Pending (Available in GSTR-2B but not in ERP):** Signifies unrecorded vendor bills or delayed receipt.
- **Missing (In ERP purchase register but absent in GSTR-2B):** Highlights non-compliance by the supplier, restricting ITC claim under rule 36(4).

## 5. ERP GST Calculation Engine Architecture

The core tax engine within the ERP must operate deterministically based on geographical location codes and product master attributes.

### 5.1 Tax Determination Logic

When a sales order or invoice is initialized, the engine executes the following decision tree:

1. **Retrieve Company State Code ($S_{comp}$):** Derived from the shipping warehouse or registered branch GSTIN.
2. **Retrieve Customer State Code ($S_{cust}$):** Derived from the bill-to/ship-to address or registered customer GSTIN.
3. **Location Matching Algorithm:**
   - If $S_{comp} == S_{cust}$: Apply **Intra-State Taxation** ($CGST = Rate / 2$, $SGST = Rate / 2$, $IGST = 0$).
   - If $S_{comp} \neq S_{cust}$: Apply **Inter-State Taxation** ($CGST = 0$, $SGST = 0$, $IGST = Rate$).
   - If Export/SEZ with payment of tax: Apply IGST; if under Bond/LUT: Apply Zero-Rated IGST with appropriate export declarations.

## 6. Database Schema and Cache Architecture

The following SQL Data Definition Language (DDL) scripts outline the relational database schema and caching structures required to implement the GST module within a modern ERP system (PostgreSQL/MySQL compatible).

### 6.1 Master Tables

```sql
-- HSN Code and Tax Rate Master
CREATE TABLE hsn_master (
    hsn_code VARCHAR(10) PRIMARY KEY,
    chapter_code VARCHAR(4) NOT NULL,
    description TEXT NOT NULL,
    gst_rate DECIMAL(5,2) NOT NULL, -- e.g., 18.00
    cgst_rate DECIMAL(5,2) NOT NULL,
    sgst_rate DECIMAL(5,2) NOT NULL,
    igst_rate DECIMAL(5,2) NOT NULL,
    cess_rate DECIMAL(5,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    effective_from DATE NOT NULL,
    effective_to DATE,
    created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Company Branch GST Profiles
CREATE TABLE company_gst_profiles (
    branch_id INT PRIMARY KEY AUTO_INCREMENT,
    branch_name VARCHAR(150) NOT NULL,
    gstin VARCHAR(15) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    state_code VARCHAR(2) NOT NULL,
    state_name VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    address TEXT NOT NULL,
    is_active BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE,
    created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);

-- Customer and Supplier GST Registry
CREATE TABLE business_partners (
    partner_id INT PRIMARY KEY AUTO_INCREMENT,
    partner_type ENUM('CUSTOMER', 'SUPPLIER', 'BOTH') NOT NULL,
    business_name VARCHAR(200) NOT NULL,
    gstin VARCHAR(15),
    state_code VARCHAR(2) NOT NULL,
    pan VARCHAR(10) NOT NULL,
    taxpayer_type ENUM('REGULAR', 'COMPOSITION', 'SEZ', 'UNREGISTERED', 'OVERSEAS') NOT NULL,
    billing_address TEXT NOT NULL,
    shipping_address TEXT,
    created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP
);
```

### 6.2 Transactional Tables (Sales & Purchases)

```sql
-- Sales Invoices Header
CREATE TABLE sales_invoices (
    invoice_id INT PRIMARY KEY AUTO_INCREMENT,
    invoice_number VARCHAR(50) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    invoice_date TIMESTAMP NOT NULL,
    branch_id INT NOT NULL,
    customer_id INT NOT NULL,
    place_of_supply_state_code VARCHAR(2) NOT NULL,
    invoice_type ENUM('B2B', 'B2C_LARGE', 'B2C_SMALL', 'EXPORT') NOT NULL,
    total_taxable_value DECIMAL(12,2) NOT NULL,
    total_cgst DECIMAL(10,2) NOT NULL,
    total_sgst DECIMAL(10,2) NOT NULL,
    total_igst DECIMAL(10,2) NOT NULL,
    total_cess DECIMAL(10,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    invoice_grand_total DECIMAL(12,2) NOT NULL,
    irn_hash VARCHAR(64),
    signed_qr_code TEXT,
    eway_bill_number VARCHAR(25),
    eway_bill_date TIMESTAMP,
    fulfillment_status VARCHAR(30) DEFAULT 'PENDING',
    created_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    FOREIGN KEY (branch_id) REFERENCES company_gst_profiles(branch_id),
    FOREIGN KEY (customer_id) REFERENCES business_partners(partner_id)
);

-- Sales Invoice Line Items
CREATE TABLE sales_invoice_items (
    item_line_id INT PRIMARY KEY AUTO_INCREMENT,
    invoice_id INT NOT NULL,
    sku_code VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    item_description TEXT NOT NULL,
    hsn_code VARCHAR(10) NOT NULL,
    quantity DECIMAL(10,2) NOT NULL,
    unit_price DECIMAL(12,2) NOT NULL,
    discount_amount DECIMAL(10,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    taxable_value DECIMAL(12,2) NOT NULL,
    gst_rate DECIMAL(5,2) NOT NULL,
    cgst_amount DECIMAL(10,2) NOT NULL,
    sgst_amount DECIMAL(10,2) NOT NULL,
    igst_amount DECIMAL(10,2) NOT NULL,
    total_item_amount DECIMAL(12,2) NOT NULL,
    FOREIGN KEY (invoice_id) REFERENCES sales_invoices(invoice_id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
    FOREIGN KEY (hsn_code) REFERENCES hsn_master(hsn_code)
);
```

### 6.3 Compliance and Cache Tables (Redis / SQL Cache)

```sql
-- GSTR-2B Inflow Cache Table for ITC Reconciliation
CREATE TABLE gstr2b_cache (
    cache_id INT PRIMARY KEY AUTO_INCREMENT,
    recipient_gstin VARCHAR(15) NOT NULL,
    supplier_gstin VARCHAR(15) NOT NULL,
    supplier_name VARCHAR(200),
    invoice_number VARCHAR(50) NOT NULL,
    invoice_date DATE NOT NULL,
    invoice_value DECIMAL(12,2) NOT NULL,
    taxable_value DECIMAL(12,2) NOT NULL,
    igst_amount DECIMAL(10,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    cgst_amount DECIMAL(10,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    sgst_amount DECIMAL(10,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    filing_status VARCHAR(20) NOT NULL, -- FILED, UNFILED
    reconciliation_status ENUM('MATCHED', 'MISMATCHED', 'PENDING', 'MISSING') DEFAULT 'PENDING',
    tax_period VARCHAR(10) NOT NULL, -- e.g., '072026'
    fetched_at TIMESTAMP DEFAULT CURRENT_TIMESTAMP,
    UNIQUE KEY unique_gstr2b_inv (recipient_gstin, supplier_gstin, invoice_number)
);

-- E-Way Bill Dispatch Log
CREATE TABLE eway_bill_logs (
    eway_bill_id INT PRIMARY KEY AUTO_INCREMENT,
    invoice_id INT NOT NULL,
    eway_bill_number VARCHAR(25) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    generated_date TIMESTAMP NOT NULL,
    valid_upto TIMESTAMP NOT NULL,
    transporter_id VARCHAR(15),
    transporter_name VARCHAR(150),
    vehicle_number VARCHAR(20),
    distance_km INT,
    status VARCHAR(20) DEFAULT 'ACTIVE',
    FOREIGN KEY (invoice_id) REFERENCES sales_invoices(invoice_id)
);
```
